## Concession and Unexpected Results — မမျှော်လင့်ထားသော ရလဒ်များ

အဆင့်မြင့် Business Documents နှင့် Part 7 Reading Passage များတွင် အထူးတလည် အသုံးများသော စကားဆက်များ ဖြစ်သည်:

| Expression | Grammar Pattern | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Even if** | `Even if + S + V` | *Even if demand falls, we will continue production.* |
| **Regardless of** | `Regardless of + noun` | *The policy applies regardless of age.* |
| **Irrespective of** | `Irrespective of + noun` | *All applicants are considered irrespective of experience.* |
| **Much as** | `Much as + S + V` | *Much as we appreciate the offer, we cannot accept it.* |
| **Admittedly** | `Sentence adverb` | *Admittedly, the initial cost is high.* |
| **Still** | `Sentence connector / adverb` | *The task was difficult. Still, we completed it.* |
| **All the same** | `Sentence connector` | *The price was high. All the same, the product sold well.* |
| **Granted that** | `Granted that + S + V` | *Granted that the proposal has benefits, its costs remain high.* |
