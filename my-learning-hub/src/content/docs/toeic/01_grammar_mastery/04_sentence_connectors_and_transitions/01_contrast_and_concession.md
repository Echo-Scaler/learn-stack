## Contrast & Concession — ဆန့်ကျင်ဘက်နှင့် ခွင့်ပြုချက်ပြ စကားဆက်များ

TOEIC Part 5 & 6 တွင် အမေးအများဆုံး စကားဆက်ဇယား ဖြစ်သည်။ အဓိပ္ပာယ်တူသော်လည်း **Clause လိုက်သလား၊ Noun လိုက်သလား၊ Sentence Connector လား** ကို ခွဲခြားရပါမည်။

| Expression | Grammar Pattern | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Although** | `Although + S + V` | *Although it was raining, we went out.* |
| **Though** | `Though + S + V` | *Though he was tired, he continued working.* |
| **Even though** | `Even though + S + V` | *Even though sales fell, profits increased.* |
| **Despite** | `Despite + noun / V-ing` | *Despite the rain, we went out.* |
| **In spite of** | `In spite of + noun / V-ing` | *In spite of being tired, he continued.* |
| **However** | `Sentence. However, sentence.` | *Sales fell. However, profits increased.* |
| **Nevertheless** | `Sentence. Nevertheless, sentence.` | *The task was difficult. Nevertheless, we finished it.* |
| **Nonetheless** | `Sentence. Nonetheless, sentence.` | *The cost was high. Nonetheless, we purchased it.* |
| **Whereas** | `Whereas + S + V` | *Sales increased, whereas profits declined.* |
| **While** | `While + S + V` | *While the product is expensive, it is reliable.* |
| **On the other hand**| `Sentence connector` | *The model is cheap. On the other hand, it is slow.* |
| **In contrast** | `Sentence connector` | *In contrast, the second model is more efficient.* |
| **Unlike** | `Unlike + noun` | *Unlike last year, sales have increased.* |
| **Even so** | `Sentence connector` | *The deadline was tight. Even so, we completed the work.* |

> [!CAUTION]
> **အဓိက သတိပြုရန်:**
> - `Despite` နောက်တွင် `of` လုံးဝ မပါရပါ (❌ Despite of the rain မဟုတ်ပါ -> ✅ Despite the rain)။
> - `However` နှင့် `Nevertheless` သည် Sentence Connectors (Adverbs) ဖြစ်သဖြင့် Comma ခံ၍ ဝါကျနှစ်ခုကြားတွင် တိုက်ရိုက် သုံးရသည်။
