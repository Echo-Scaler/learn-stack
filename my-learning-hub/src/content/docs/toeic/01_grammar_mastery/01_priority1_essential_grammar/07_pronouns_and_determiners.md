## ၁။ Pronoun Declension (နာမ်စားဇယား)

| Subject (主格) | Object (目的格) | Possessive Adj (所有格) | Possessive Pronoun (所有代名詞) | Reflexive (再帰代名詞) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| I | me | my + Noun | mine | myself |
| You | you | your + Noun | yours | yourself |
| He | him | his + Noun | his | himself |
| She | her | her + Noun | hers | herself |
| It | it | its + Noun | — | itself |
| We | us | our + Noun | ours | ourselves |
| They | them | their + Noun | theirs | themselves |

---

## ၂။ TOEIC စာမေးပွဲ အသုံးများသော စည်းမျဉ်းများ

1. **Possessive Adjective + Noun:**
   - *Ms. Taylor presented [ **her** ] proposal to the executive board.*
2. **by + Reflexive Pronoun (= alone / without help):**
   - *The technician repaired the equipment [ **by himself** ].*
3. **Those who + Plural Verb (= people who):**
   - *[ **Those** ] who wish to attend the seminar must register online.*
