## ၁။ TOEIC တွင် မဖြစ်မနေသိရမည့် ကာလ ၆ မျိုးနှင့် Time Markers

| ကာလ (Tense) | ပုံသေနည်း (Formula) | အဓိက အချိန်ပြစကားလုံး (Key Time Markers) | နမူနာ ဝါကျ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Simple Present** | V1 / V-s/es | regularly, routinely, often, every month, typically | *The team meets every Monday.* |
| **Simple Past** | V2 (-ed) | yesterday, last week, in 2022, ... ago, previously | *Sales rose significantly last quarter.* |
| **Simple Future** | will + V1 / be going to | tomorrow, next year, soon, upcoming, shortly | *The director will announce the decision soon.* |
| **Present Perfect** | have/has + V3 | since, for + duration, already, recently, lately | *We have expanded operations since 2020.* |
| **Past Perfect** | had + V3 | before, by the time (+ past action) | *The plane had departed before we arrived.* |
| **Future Perfect** | will have + V3 | by next Friday, by the end of this month | *She will have completed the project by June.* |

## ၂။ စာမေးပွဲ ထောင်ချောက်: Time and Condition Clauses

အချိန်နှင့် အခြေအနေပြ စကားဆက်များ (`when, after, before, as soon as, if, unless`) ပါဝင်သော Clause တွင် **Future Tense (will) ကို မသုံးဘဲ Simple Present Tense ကိုသာ သုံးရသည်**:
- ❌ *When the shipment will arrive, we will inspect it.*
- ✅ *When the shipment [ **arrives** ], we will inspect it.*
