## ၁။ Parallel Structure ဆိုတာဘာလဲ?

ဝါကျတစ်ခုအတွင်း ချိတ်ဆက်ထားသော အစိတ်အပိုင်းများသည် **တူညီသော သဒ္ဒါပုံစံ (Grammatical Form)** ရှိရမည်:
- Noun and Noun
- Adjective and Adjective
- Gerund and Gerund (V-ing and V-ing)
- Infinitive and Infinitive (to-V and to-V)

---

## ၂။ TOEIC နမူနာများ

- ❌ *The job requires patience, intelligence, and to be creative.*
- ✅ *The job requires **patience**, **intelligence**, and [ **creativity** ].* (Noun, Noun, and Noun)

- ❌ *She enjoys analyzing market trends and to forecast sales.*
- ✅ *She enjoys **analyzing** market trends and [ **forecasting** ] sales.* (V-ing and V-ing)
